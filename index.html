ABOUT US

Who We Are

Heshima Progressive Group is a youth-focused initiative bringing together people aged 18–35 across local communities to identify, confront and resolve pressing social and economic challenges.

Become Part of the Change →
◎

Our Mission

To unite young people aged 18–35 across local communities to identify, confront, and resolve pressing social and economic challenges — building skills, opportunity, and collective responsibility through action, mentorship, and civic participation.

◉

Our Vision

A generation of empowered, self-reliant youth leading resilient, respected, and thriving communities across Kenya.
