WHO WE ARE

Progress begins when people move together.

Heshima Progressive Group is a youth-focused community movement committed to turning ideas, skills and collective responsibility into meaningful action.

01

A platform for possibility.

We believe young people should not simply wait for opportunities. They should be equipped to create them, participate in their communities and help shape the future they want to live in.

Heshima creates space for young people to learn, collaborate, lead initiatives and respond to the social and economic challenges affecting their communities.

Explore our focus
02
OUR MISSION

From challenges to collective action.

To unite young people aged 18–35 across local communities to identify, confront, and resolve pressing social and economic challenges — building skills, opportunity, and collective responsibility through action, mentorship, and civic participation.

03
OUR VISION

A generation prepared to lead.

A generation of empowered, self-reliant youth leading resilient, respected and thriving communities.
