ABOUT US

Who We Are

Heshima Progressive Group is a movement of young people united around shared values of dignity, service and collective growth. We are driven by the conviction that our communities have everything they need to solve their own problems and realize their own futures—if we work together.

Founded on the principles of unity and practical action, Heshima engages youth in community development initiatives that produce real, visible change.

OUR FOCUS

What We Do

We focus on practical areas where collective action creates immediate and lasting impact.

Education & Skills

Building pathways to opportunity through training and mentorship programs that equip young people with skills for success.

Community Infrastructure

Investing in the physical foundations of thriving communities—from water systems to market improvements.

Economic Empowerment

Creating sustainable livelihoods and economic opportunity through youth enterprise and cooperative ventures.

Civic Participation

Strengthening democratic participation and accountability so communities have a voice in decisions that affect them.

OUR PROGRAMS

From Ideas to Action

Our programs are designed by and for young people, rooted in the needs and dreams of the communities we serve.

Youth Leadership Academy

An intensive program developing the next generation of community leaders through skills training, mentorship and practical projects.

Community Development Corps

Young people working full-time on community-identified development priorities—from infrastructure to social services.

Youth Enterprise Network

Supporting young entrepreneurs with business training, startup capital and market access to create sustainable livelihoods.

Civic Engagement Initiative

Building youth power to influence local policy and hold institutions accountable to community needs.
